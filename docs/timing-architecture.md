# Dubline timing & alignment architecture — exhaustive inventory

*All paths relative to `/home/brad/code/Dubline`. Line numbers are from the files as they exist now.*

## 1. Narrative: the end-to-end timing model

The **timing grid is the source media's own clock**, in seconds, after an optional range trim. When a section is selected, `run_pipeline` re-cuts the container to `selected-source.mkv` with `-ss/-t … -avoid_negative_ts make_zero` (`pipeline.py:266-272`), so from that point on *every* number in the job — cues, windows, references, the render timeline, the mix, QC — lives on a new zero-based clock, and `duration` (`pipeline.py:276`) is the canonical length of that clock. The only place the original clock reappears is the whole-film speaker registry, where cue copies are shifted by `+range_start` for diarization and then thrown away (`pipeline.py:532-535, 556-562`), and the whole-film face registry, which scans `original_source` (`pipeline.py:360-365`).

Timing *evidence* enters from three independent sources: the forced aligner's per-word start/end (`qwen_asr_worker.py:57-60`), Whisper's segment/word timestamps in the fallback path (`whisper_worker.py:43-53`), and subtitle card timestamps (`subtitles.py:152-181`). These are reconciled into a single cue list where the **cue span `[start,end]` becomes the one timing object the rest of the pipeline uses** (`pipeline.py:1172-1241`). Word grouping into cues is decided purely by silence and punctuation heuristics — gap > 0.62 s, duration > 10 s, or sentence-final punctuation after 0.7 s (`qwen_asr_worker.py:42`) — and cues are then re-grouped into *utterances* on a same-speaker / gap ≤ 1.2 s / ≤ 12 s rule (`dialogue.py:216-241`), explicitly so that translation and voicing operate over a whole span "pauses included".

From there the span is reinterpreted three more times. It becomes a **target speaking duration**: `cue_seconds` plus slack borrowed from the silence before the next cue, capped at 12 % of the span if the mouth is visible and 45 % if not (`pipeline.py:707-715`). It becomes a **word budget** for the LLM adapter: `target * 2.65` words, backed by `predicted_seconds = words / 2.65` (`adapter_worker.py:16-18, 212`). And it becomes a **hard fit constraint**: `audio_fit.fit_audio` resamples/stretches/pads every take to exactly `target` seconds (`audio_fit.py:65-139`).

Nothing ever aligns the *generated* speech to the *source* speech beyond that single scalar duration. IndexTTS receives one `duration_factor` derived from character count (`tts.py:104-105`); if the take still overruns by > 8 % the worker re-synthesizes once with an inverse-scaled target (`tts_worker.py:40-56`); whatever survives is then time-stretched with rubberband phrase-by-phrase and pause-widened to fill the span (`audio_fit.py:73-124`). Placement is a single memcpy at `round(start * 24000)` (`pipeline.py:1505`), and the source dialogue is silenced only over the *source* word spans ± 35 ms with 25 ms fades (`pipeline.py:1481-1498`). Visual evidence is a single boolean per cue (`mouth_visible`) computed by thresholding mouth-pixel motion aggregated across the whole cue window (`visual_speakers_worker.py:243-257`), and it acts as a *timing* threshold everywhere: 5 % vs 8 % stretch tolerance (`pipeline.py:785`, `qc.py:169`), slack cap, adapter scoring weights (`adapter_worker.py:76-82`), and lip-sync eligibility (`lipsync.py:79-87`). QC closes the loop by comparing the container duration to `duration` within 250 ms (`qc.py:337`) — the only end-to-end sync check in the system.

## 4. Conflated quantities

1. **Source ASR segment span ⇄ speaker turn ⇄ target speech duration.** `merge_into_utterances` (`dialogue.py:243`) sets `previous["end"] = cue["end"]`, deliberately swallowing internal pauses; the docstring (`:219-223`) says synthesis must be "placed over the whole span (pauses included)". That merged span becomes `cue_seconds` (`pipeline.py:707`) and then the exact frame count `fit_audio` must fill (`audio_fit.py:68`). A pause the actor took is thereby converted into speech time the dub must occupy, which `fit_audio` re-invents as artificial inter-phrase gaps of up to 1.2 s (`audio_fit.py:90-99`). A speaker *turn* is never measured directly: `merge_into_utterances` proxies it with `speaker_id` equality plus a 1.2 s gap, and diarization turn boundaries (`diarization.py:73-76`) only vote on labels — they never move a cue edge.
2. **Visible mouth motion interval ⇄ per-cue boolean ⇄ phoneme-timing strictness.** `mouth_visible` is a single bool thresholded from motion aggregated over the whole cue window (`visual_speakers_worker.py:243-257`), then OR-ed across merged fragments (`dialogue.py:249`) while the underlying confidences are min-ed (`:256-257`). That bool then decides *timing* policy: slack `0.12` vs `0.45` (`pipeline.py:713`), stretch tolerance 5 % vs 8 % (`pipeline.py:785`, `qc.py:169`, `adapter_worker.py:201`), and adapter viseme weighting (`adapter_worker.py:76-82`). "A mouth was visibly moving somewhere in this span" is being used as "phoneme timing must match here".
3. **Target natural speech duration ⇄ slot length.** `target = cue_seconds + min(gap, slack_cap)` (`pipeline.py:714`) fuses "how long the line naturally takes" with "how much room exists". It is passed to IndexTTS as `duration_factor` (`tts.py:105`), to the adapter as a word budget (`adapter_worker.py:212`), to `fit_audio` as a hard length, and to the short-fill QC as a denominator (`pipeline.py:834-836`) — one scalar wearing four hats.
4. **Subtitle card timing ⇄ speech timing.** `reconcile_subtitles_with_asr` blends card edges with ASR word edges clamped to `card ± 0.5 s` (`pipeline.py:1195-1196`); the resulting cue span is neither. The true card times survive as `subtitle_start`/`subtitle_end` (`:1218`) and are never read again.
5. **Take length ⇄ published cue length.** The rendered take may run up to 45 % past `cue["end"]` (slack), but the exported SRT (`pipeline.py:1753`), the EDL (`:1770`), the lip-sync clip window (`lipsync.py:104-105`) and `analyze_performance`'s measurement window all still use `cue["end"]`. Audio placed by `render_timeline` (`:1505-1506`) can therefore extend past the regenerated mouth.
6. **Text length ⇄ duration.** `predicted_seconds` (`adapter_worker.py:16-18`) and IndexTTS's `estimated = len(text)/14.0` (`tts.py:104`) are two different, mutually inconsistent text→time models, both used as if they were phoneme timing.
7. **Voice activity ⇄ mouth activity.** `active_fill_percent` (`audio_fit.py:137`) measures generated-voice-on time against the *span*, and `pipeline.py:839-840` interprets a low value as "the mouth is moving in silence" — a visual claim inferred purely from audio.

## 5. Gaps

**Computed but unused.** Per-word timestamps survive the whole pipeline (`dialogue.py:248` concatenates them) yet are consumed in exactly three places: card assignment (`pipeline.py:1176-1185`), source muting (`:1481-1487`), and a *count* in `viseme_score` (`adapter_worker.py:50`). Word `probability` (`qwen_asr_worker.py:60`, `whisper_worker.py:46`) feeds cue-creation confidence only. On the visual side, `mouth_motion`, `mouth_visibility`, `single_face_ratio`, `distinct_face_tracks` (`visual_speakers_worker.py:251-255`) are written to JSON and read by nothing; the per-frame motion series is collapsed to a 70th percentile (`:236`) and discarded, and `face_area_ratio` is read only by the lip-sync gate (`lipsync.py:84`). `source_performance.energy_contour` and `pause_ratio` (`dialogue.py:169`) are compared *after* synthesis (`qc.py:212-226`) and used only as a prior in `rhythm_score` (`adapter_worker.py:44`) — never to shape the take. `subtitle_start`/`subtitle_end` are dead. `pipeline.fit_audio` (`:1432-1437`), a whole-line rubberband path, is dead code superseded by `audio_fit.fit_audio`; `make_reference`'s `padding` argument is neutralised by `min(.08, padding)` (`:1417-1418`), so the caller's `0.15`/`0.8` never take effect.

**Missing entirely.** There are no **target-language phoneme or word timestamps** — nothing ever force-aligns the generated take, so the only feedback is scalar (`raw_duration`, `stretch_percent`, `active_fill_percent`) and text-level (`word_similarity`, WER/CER from `qc.py:66-70`). There is no **shot/cut detection**: ASR windows come from silence (`pipeline.py:1377-1391`), and the lip-sync overlay enables on `between(t,start,end)` (`lipsync.py:148`) with no guarantee the span lies inside one shot — the ±0.12 s pad and 120 ms audio delay (`:103,117`) are the only nod to boundaries. There is no **mouth-open energy/aperture time series**, so phrase placement inside `fit_audio` (`:88-99`) spreads pauses evenly rather than toward actual mouth activity, despite the comment at `audio_fit.py:74-76` claiming the opposite intent. There is no **per-word source→target correspondence**, no **drift or A/V-sync measurement** of the finished mix against picture (only container duration ± 250 ms, `qc.py:337`), no **video fps propagation** into exports (EDL is hardcoded 24 fps at `pipeline.py:1765` even though `video_fps` exists at `:1244-1251`), and no check that a slack-extended take overruns real source audio events in the following gap — the only guard is the fixed 0.15 s margin at `pipeline.py:712`.
